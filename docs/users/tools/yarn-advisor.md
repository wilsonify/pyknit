# Yarn Advisor

[Open Yarn Advisor](../../demos/yarn-advisor/demo.html)

## What it does

Recommends yarn weight and fiber type for your project based on your preferences
for drape, warmth, and intended use.

## How to use it

1. **Project type** -- What are you making? (scarf, hat, sock, sweater, etc.)
2. **Target gauge** -- What yarn weight does your pattern call for?
3. **Fabric drape** -- How stiff or drapey should the fabric be?
4. **Warmth** -- How warm does the finished item need to be?
5. **Fiber preference** -- Any fiber you prefer or want to avoid?
6. **Intended use** -- Everyday wear, gift, baby item, outdoor gear?
7. Click **Get Recommendations**

## Reading the results

- **Best fiber** -- Top recommendation with score (0-100) and confidence level
- **Alternatives** -- Other good options ranked by score
- **Tradeoffs** -- Honest pros and cons for each recommendation

## Example

**Input:** Sweater, worsted gauge, balanced drape, warm, merino preferred, everyday
**Result:** Merino wool scores highest (92/100, high confidence). Alternatives:
alpaca blend (85/100) for extra warmth, superwash merino (83/100) for easy care.

## Tips

- Be specific about drape -- "structured" for cables, "drapey" for lace
- Consider care requirements if you hate hand-washing
- Baby items: always check for hypoallergenic fibers
