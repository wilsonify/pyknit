# Needle Advisor

[Open Needle Advisor](../../demos/needle-advisor/demo.html)

## What it does

Recommends needle size (mm and US), needle type, and cable length based on your
yarn, project, and construction method.

## How to use it

1. **Yarn weight** -- What weight yarn are you using?
2. **Target gauge** -- How many stitches per inch? (from your gauge swatch)
3. **Project type** -- What are you making?
4. **Fabric density** -- Loose, balanced, or dense fabric?
5. **Construction** -- How will you knit it? (flat, circular, DPNs, small circumference)
6. Click **Get Recommendation**

## Reading the results

- **Recommended size** -- Exact mm and US size to use
- **Size range** -- Acceptable range if you can't find the exact size
- **Needle types** -- Straight, circular, DPNs, or interchangeables
- **Cable length** -- Recommended cable length for circular needles

## Common constructions

| Construction | Needles | Best for |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| Flat | Straight or circular | Scarves, blanket squares |
| Circular (seamless) | Circular, magic loop or fixed | Hats, sweater bodies |
| DPNs | Double-pointed | Socks, sleeves, mittens |
| Small circumference | 9" circular or two circulars | Socks, hat crowns |

## Tips

- Always knit a gauge swatch with your recommended needle size
- If your gauge is too loose, go down one needle size
- If your gauge is too tight, go up one needle size
